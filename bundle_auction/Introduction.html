Experiment overview

The experiment consists of 2 practice rounds followed by 12 trading rounds.

Your assigned role for all rounds:
{{ if is_buyer }} Buyer {{ else }} Seller {{ endif }}

Market Rules:
{{ if treatment == 'baseline' }} You will be trading products representing two distinct goods, labelled as Product A and Product B. Each type of Product is bought and sold in separate markets, and you can choose to trade either Product at any point during the round. {{ endif }} {{ if treatment == 'single_package' }} You will be trading packages containing a combination of two distinct goods, labelled as Product A and Product B. Each Package contains exactly 1 unit of each Product (1:1 ratio), and you can choose to trade Packages at any point during the round. {{ endif }} {{ if treatment == 'package_menu' }} You will be trading packages containing a combination of two distinct goods, labelled as Product A and Product B. Each type of Package is bought and sold in separate markets, and you can choose to trade either Package at any point during the round. Note that each Package contains a different amount of each Product, and these amounts may change between rounds. {{ endif }}
Trading Guidelines:
Your primary objective is to maximise your total profit.
    {{ if is_buyer }}
  • As a Buyer, try to purchase units at a price lower than your Value.
    • {{ else }}
  • As a Seller, try to sell units at a price higher than your Cost.
    • {{ endif }}
